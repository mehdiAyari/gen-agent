# Expenses

How to get back what you paid for Fjord Labs.

## What is covered

- Travel for work: train in second class, or a plane when the train takes more than six hours.
- Meals during a work trip: up to **35 euros a day**.
- Hotels: up to **150 euros a night** in Oslo, Paris and London, **110 euros** elsewhere.
- Books and online courses related to your job: up to **500 euros a year**, without asking first.

Taxis are covered only after 22:00 or when no public transport goes there.

## How to claim

Send each expense in the Expenses app within **30 days**, with a photo of the receipt. Claims are paid with the next
salary when they arrive before the 20th of the month.

Expenses above 1,000 euros need the approval of your manager before you pay them.
