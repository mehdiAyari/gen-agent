# Remote work at Fjord Labs

Fjord Labs is a fictional software company of 140 people, with offices in Oslo and Lyon. This page is part of its
employee handbook.

## Where you can work from

Every employee may work from home, or from anywhere else, up to **three days a week**. Teams agree on one shared
office day, usually Tuesday, so that everyone meets in person at least once a week.

Working from another country is allowed for up to **20 working days a year**, inside the European Economic Area only.
Ask your manager at least two weeks before you leave.

## Equipment

Each employee gets a **home office budget of 600 euros**, once every three years, for a chair, a desk or a screen.
The laptop, its charger and a headset are provided by the IT team and stay the property of Fjord Labs.

## Core hours

Meetings are only scheduled between **10:00 and 16:00**, Central European Time. Outside core hours, nobody is expected
to answer messages.
