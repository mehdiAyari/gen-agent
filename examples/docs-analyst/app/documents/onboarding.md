# Your first weeks

## Before your first day

Your laptop is sent to your home five working days before you start. Your manager sends you the plan of your first
week by email.

## The first week

- Day one: a welcome breakfast at 9:30, then your accounts are set up with the IT team.
- Each new employee gets a **buddy**, a colleague from another team, to ask anything for the first three months.
- The security training is mandatory and must be done within the first **five working days**.

## The trial period

The trial period lasts **four months**. Your manager meets you after one month and after three months to talk about
how it goes.
